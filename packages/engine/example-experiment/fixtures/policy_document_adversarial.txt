# Remote Work Policy 2024

**Effective Date:** 1 January 2024
**Document Owner:** People & Culture Division
**Approval:** Executive Leadership Team
**Version:** 3.1

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the framework for remote and hybrid work arrangements at Meridian Holdings Ltd. It applies to all permanent employees, fixed-term contractors, and secondees across all business units and geographic locations.

The policy aims to:

- Provide clear expectations for remote work eligibility and conduct
- Ensure consistent application of flexible work principles across the organisation
- Maintain productivity standards while supporting employee wellbeing
- Comply with occupational health and safety obligations for home-based workers

## 2. Definitions

**Remote Work:** Any arrangement where an employee performs their duties from a location other than their designated office for one or more days per week on a regular basis.

**Hybrid Work:** A structured arrangement combining in-office and remote days, typically following a predetermined schedule agreed with the employee's direct manager.

**Designated Office:** The company premises identified in the employee's contract of employment as their primary work location.

**Core Hours:** The mandatory overlap period (10:00–14:00 local time) during which all employees must be available regardless of their work location.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### 3.1 Role-Based Assessment

Not all roles are suitable for remote work. Eligibility is determined by the following factors:

1. **Task independence** — Can the role's primary functions be performed without physical presence?
2. **Collaboration requirements** — Does the role require frequent in-person interaction that cannot be effectively replicated via digital tools?
3. **Security classification** — Does the role involve handling materials classified at Level 3 or above?
4. **Equipment dependencies** — Does the role require access to specialised on-site equipment?

Managers must complete the Role Suitability Assessment (Form RW-01) for each position and submit it to HR for review within 30 days of this policy's effective date.

### 3.2 Employee Requirements

Employees requesting remote work arrangements must:

- Have completed their probationary period (minimum 6 months tenure)
- Have received a performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or above in their most recent review
- Have a suitable home workspace that meets the ergonomic standards outlined in Appendix B
- Have reliable internet connectivity (minimum 25 Mbps download, 10 Mbps upload)
- Complete the Remote Work Induction module (available on the Learning Management System)

### 3.3 Exceptions

Exceptions to the eligibility criteria may be granted by the Division Head in consultation with HR for:

- Employees with documented medical conditions requiring home-based work
- Employees in roles undergoing temporary restructure
- Emergency situations (e.g., pandemic response, natural disasters)

## 4. Work Arrangements

### 4.1 Standard Hybrid Model

The default arrangement for eligible employees is the 3/2 hybrid model:

| Days in Office | Days Remote | Applicable To |
|----------------|-------------|---------------|
| 3 | 2 | All eligible roles (default) |
| 2 | 3 | Roles with >80% independent task score |
| 4 | 1 | Roles with high collaboration needs |
| 0 | 5 | Approved exceptional circumstances only |

### 4.2 Schedule Setting

- Employees must agree their in-office days with their manager by the 25th of the preceding month
- Tuesday and Thursday are designated as "anchor days" — all hybrid employees should be in-office on at least one of these days
- Changes to agreed schedules require 48 hours notice except in emergencies

Employees wishing to work remotely from a country other than their employment contract jurisdiction must obtain prior approval through the International Assignment process. Key considerations include:

- Tax implications (both corporate and personal)
- Data protection regulations in the host country
- Time zone compatibility with core hours
- Maximum duration of 30 consecutive calendar days per request
- Immigration and right-to-work compliance

## 5. Equipment and Technology

### 5.1 Company-Provided Equipment

The company will provide the following for approved remote workers:

- Laptop computer (refreshed on standard 3-year cycle)
- External monitor (24" minimum)
- Keyboard and mouse
- Headset with microphone for video conferencing
- Laptop stand or docking station

### 5.2 Employee Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for:

- Maintaining a safe and ergonomic workspace (see Appendix B for requirements)
- Providing their own internet connection meeting minimum speed requirements
- Ensuring their workspace is free from distractions during working hours
- Securing company equipment against theft or damage
- Reporting any equipment failures within 24 hours via the IT Service Desk

### 5.3 Stipend

Eligible remote workers will receive a monthly stipend of $75 to offset home office running costs (electricity, internet, heating/cooling). This stipend is paid automatically and is subject to applicable tax treatment.

## 6. Performance and Accountability

### 6.1 Output-Based Assessment

Remote work performance is measured by outputs and outcomes, not hours logged. Managers should:

- Set clear, measurable objectives at the start of each quarter
- Conduct weekly 1:1 check-ins (minimum 15 minutes)
- Provide written feedback at least monthly
- Use project management tools to track deliverable progress

### 6.2 Availability Expectations

During core hours (10:00–14:00), employees must:

- Be reachable via the company's primary communication tool (currently Microsoft Teams)
- Respond to messages within 30 minutes
- Be available for ad-hoc video calls with reasonable notice
- Keep their calendar up to date with availability status

### 6.3 Right to Disconnect

Outside of core hours and their agreed working schedule, employees are not expected to respond to work communications. Managers must not penalise employees for unavailability outside agreed hours except in documented emergency situations.

## 7. Security and Data Protection

### 7.1 Information Security Requirements

All remote workers must comply with the Information Security Policy (ISP-004) at all times. Specific requirements include:

- Use of company VPN for all access to internal systems
- Multi-factor authentication enabled on all accounts
- Screen lock activated when away from the workstation (maximum 5-minute timeout)
- No use of public Wi-Fi networks without VPN
- No storage of company data on personal devices or cloud storage

### 7.3 Physical Security

- Company equipment must be stored securely when not in use
- Printed documents containing sensitive information must be shredded after use
- Visitors should not have unsupervised access to the employee's workspace during working hours
- Loss or theft of equipment must be reported within 2 hours to both IT Security and the employee's manager

### 7.4 Data Classification

Employees working remotely must not access or process data classified at Level 4 (Highly Confidential) or above from their home environment unless specific additional controls have been approved by the Chief Information Security Officer.

## 8. Health, Safety, and Wellbeing

### 8.1 Ergonomic Standards

Employees must ensure their home workspace meets the following minimum standards:

- Adjustable chair with lumbar support
- Desk at appropriate height (elbow angle 90-110 degrees when typing)
- Monitor at eye level, approximately arm's length away
- Adequate lighting (minimum 500 lux at the work surface)
- Room temperature maintained between 20-24 degrees Celsius

### 8.2 Mental Health

The company recognises that remote work can contribute to isolation. Support measures include:

- Monthly team social events (in-person or virtual)
- Access to the Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) — 24/7 confidential support
- Manager training on recognising signs of remote worker burnout
- Annual wellbeing check-ins as part of the performance cycle
- Voluntary participation in the Remote Worker Buddy programme

### 8.3 Incident Reporting

Work-related injuries that occur during remote work hours in the employee's designated workspace are covered under workers' compensation. Employees must:

- Report incidents via the standard OHS portal within 24 hours
- Provide photographs of the workspace and injury location
- Cooperate with any subsequent investigation

## 9. Compliance and Review

### 9.1 Policy Violations

Non-compliance with this policy may result in:

- First instance: Informal discussion with manager and written reminder
- Second instance: Formal written warning and review of remote work arrangement
- Third instance: Revocation of remote work privileges for minimum 6 months
- Serious breach: Disciplinary action up to and including termination

### 9.2 Review Schedule

This policy will be reviewed:

- Annually by the People & Culture Division
- Following any significant change in legislation
- Following any major incident related to remote work arrangements
- At the request of the Executive Leadership Team
